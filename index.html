Find calm and stability abroad

Short exercises and useful resources created for Ukrainians living abroad. This website is designed to support those who feel stressed in a new country. Here you can find simple practices to calm your mind, reconnect with your body, feel more grounded, and explore helpful links for mental health and everyday stability.

Why mental health matters

When you live abroad, stress builds up faster than you expect. A new environment, new rules, and language barriers all add pressure. Your mind works harder every day, even if you don’t realize it.

Paying attention to your emotional state is not a luxury. It’s the foundation: sleep, concentration, energy, decision-making, and connection with others all depend on inner calm. Small steps toward stability make your life abroad easier and safer.

This project offers short exercises that help you come back into your body, reduce anxiety, and feel more in control.

4–4 Breathing Technique

The 4–4 breathing technique is a simple exercise that helps calm the nervous system and reduce stress. You inhale for 4 seconds, then exhale for 4 seconds. This rhythm slows down your heartbeat, relaxes the body, and brings the mind back into balance. Use this practice when you feel anxious, overwhelmed, or need a moment to reset.
